The concept

A real harness. Your hands.
A sky that responds.

The visitor climbs into an authentic harness suspended in a rig. In front of them: a VR headset or a wrap-around projection wall. They reach up, grip the brake handles, and the wing reacts exactly as it would in the air.

1

The harness & risers

A genuine school harness on a gentle suspension frame. Real brake toggles on real lines — the exhibit's tactile heart, so the body remembers it.

2

Hands become controls

Pull the right line, the wing banks right. Pull both, you flare and slow. Hand & line position is tracked and fed straight into the flight model.

3

The sky responds

A real-time flight simulation renders the glide — thermals lift you, the valley sweeps below, the horizon tilts as you turn.

The 2-minute journey

What the visitor lives through

Clip in

An attendant seats the visitor and clips the harness. The screen shows a launch slope; wind ripples the grass. Anticipation.

Take off

They lean forward, the wing inflates overhead, and the ground falls away. The first lift is the moment everyone remembers.

Find the thermal

Subtle audio and a vario beep guide them to rising air. Pull a turn, circle, and climb — they're actively flying, not watching.

The glide

A wide ridge-soaring run over a signature local landscape — the postcard shot, the photo they'll want.

Touch down

They flare both brakes and land softly. A final frame offers a shareable snapshot of their flight path.

It's already proven elsewhere

The pieces exist — we combine them

Full-body flight VR rigs, paragliding training software and hand-tracked controls are all mature, shipped products. Our exhibit assembles them around a real harness for a museum context.

Realism scales with budget

Three ways to build it

The same core idea — harness, hands, responsive sky — at three fidelity levels. Start lean, upgrade later; the rig and software are modular.

Tier 1 · Lean
Entry
fastest to install
  • Static harness rig
  • Single large projection / TV wall
  • Webcam or simple sensor hand tracking
  • Pre-built sim (existing paragliding game / Birdly-style loop)
  • One fixed scenic route
Feel: arcade-fun, clearly an exhibit. Convincing enough to delight casual visitors.
Tier 2 · Recommended
Mid
best value / wow ratio
  • Suspended harness with slight sway & lean sensing
  • VR headset or curved wide projection
  • Dedicated hand/line tracking on real toggles
  • Tuned flight model with thermals & wind
  • Local landscape, spatial audio, vario sound
  • Shareable flight snapshot
Feel: genuinely immersive. Visitors describe it as "actually flying."
Tier 3 · Flagship
High
signature attraction
  • Motion platform: pitch, roll & thermal bumps
  • Wind fans scaled to airspeed
  • High-fidelity real-flight aerodynamics
  • Photoreal local terrain & weather
  • Multiple routes + instructor / race modes
  • Operator dashboard & analytics
Feel: near-training-grade. A destination exhibit people travel for.
