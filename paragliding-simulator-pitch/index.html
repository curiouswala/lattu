The concept

A real harness. Your hands.
A sky that responds.

The visitor climbs into an authentic harness suspended in a rig. In front of them: a VR headset or a wrap-around projection. They reach up, grip the brake toggles, and the wing reacts exactly as it would in the air.

1

The harness & risers

A genuine school harness on a gentle suspension frame. Real brake toggles on real lines — the exhibit's tactile heart, so the body remembers it.

2

Hands become controls

Pull the right line, the wing banks right. Pull both, you flare and slow. Hand and line position is tracked and fed straight into the flight model.

3

The sky responds

A real-time flight simulation renders the glide — thermals lift you, the valley sweeps below, the horizon tilts as you turn.

The 2-minute journey

What the visitor lives through

Clip in

An attendant seats the visitor and clips the harness. The screen shows a launch slope; wind ripples the grass. Anticipation.

Take off

They lean forward, the wing inflates overhead, and the ground falls away. The first lift is the moment everyone remembers.

Find the thermal

Audio and a vario beep guide them to rising air. Pull a turn, circle, and climb — they're actively flying, not watching.

The glide

A wide ridge-soaring run over a signature local landscape — the postcard shot, the photo they'll want.

Touch down

They flare both brakes and land softly. A final frame offers a shareable snapshot of their flight path.

Realism scales with budget

Three ways to build it

The same core idea — harness, hands, responsive sky — at three fidelity levels, each mapped to the real products above. Start lean, upgrade later; rig and software are modular.

Tier 1 · Lean
Entry
fastest to install
  • Static harness rig
  • Single large projection / TV wall
  • Webcam / sensor hand tracking
  • Existing sim (Paragliding Sim / XCSIM loop)
  • One fixed scenic route
Feel: arcade-fun, clearly an exhibit. Convincing enough to delight casual visitors.
Tier 2 · Recommended
Mid
best wow-per-euro
  • Suspended harness with lean sensing
  • VR headset or curved wide projection
  • Dedicated line/hand tracking on real toggles
  • Tuned model (WaggaSim-class soft-body)
  • Local landscape, spatial audio, vario
  • Shareable flight snapshot
Feel: genuinely immersive — Enversed / Birdly class. Visitors say "I was actually flying."
Tier 3 · Flagship
High
signature attraction
  • 6-DOF motion platform (VART-class) + wind fans & mist
  • Pitch, roll & thermal-bump cueing
  • High-fidelity real aerodynamics
  • Photoreal local terrain & weather
  • Giant suspended-sphere / dome projection
  • Multi-route, instructor & race modes + operator dashboard
Feel: near-training-grade Gasometer-scale spectacle. A destination people travel for.
